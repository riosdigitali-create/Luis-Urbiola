Entry requirements

What you need to bring

English & short technology courses
National identity document (DNI) or passport · one passport photograph · completed level test or diagnostic · first instalment. Applicants under 18 require a guardian signature. No academic prerequisites.
Bachelor degrees
Certified secondary-school completion certificate · DNI or passport · birth certificate · four photographs · entrance examination (mathematics, verbal reasoning, general knowledge) and a personal interview. Transfers from other universities may apply for credit recognition of up to 60%.
Master degrees
Bachelor degree certified by SUNEDU · curriculum vitae · two professional references · letter of motivation (600 words) · admission interview. Minimum two years of professional experience for the Executive MBA.
Doctorates
Master degree certified by SUNEDU · research proposal (2,500 words) · two academic references · interview with the doctoral committee · demonstrable B2 English. Publication record considered favourably.
International applicants
Apostilled academic documents · valid passport · student visa support letter issued by our international office · proof of financial means. We assist with the Migraciones process at no charge.
