The AI classroom: what eighteen months of machine tutoring did to Latin American education

Between January 2025 and June 2026 we placed an AI tutor inside 412 course sections across three schools and measured what happened. Completion rose. Attendance did not. Written assessment stopped meaning what it used to mean. This is the full account, including the parts that did not work.

Dr. Quispe examines the evidence from Perú, Chile, Colombia and México, sets it against the OECD's 2026 figures, and argues that the real disruption is not cheating but the collapse of the essay as a measuring instrument. He proposes a replacement: oral defence at scale, made affordable by the same technology that broke the old model.

Director of Artificial Intelligence · PhD Machine Learning, ex-Google Cloud
