Institutional accreditation

Who licenses us, and what each licence means

Instituto y Universidad Luis Urbiola operates under three Peruvian authorities and eight international partnership agreements. Every registration number below can be checked against the public registers of the issuing body.

MINEDU — Ministerio de Educación

Our institute has held an operating licence from the Peruvian Ministry of Education since 1994, renewed most recently in 2023. The licence authorises us to deliver technical and continuing-education programmes and to issue institutional certificates with legal standing throughout Perú.

  • Issuing body: Ministerio de Educación del Perú
  • Scope: Technical and continuing education across seven authorised campuses
  • Valid until: 31 December 2028

SUNEDU — Resolution 148-2019

Our university licence was granted by the Superintendencia Nacional de Educación Superior Universitaria under Resolution 148-2019, after an audit of infrastructure, faculty qualifications, research output and financial sustainability. It is the resolution that makes every bachelor, master and doctoral degree we award legally valid.

  • Issuing body: Superintendencia Nacional de Educación Superior Universitaria
  • Scope: All bachelor, master and doctoral degrees, registered nationally
  • Valid until: 14 March 2031

SINEACE — Quality accreditation

Accreditation by the Sistema Nacional de Evaluación, Acreditación y Certificación de la Calidad Educativa is voluntary in Perú and held by a minority of institutions. It certifies our curriculum design, student outcomes, graduate employability and continuous-improvement process, and is renewed on a cyclical review.

  • Issuing body: SINEACE — Sistema Nacional de Evaluación, Acreditación y Certificación
  • Scope: Programme-level quality accreditation and graduate outcomes
  • Valid until: 30 June 2029

Cambridge Assessment — Centre PE-084

We are an authorised Cambridge English examination centre. Candidates prepare, register and sit A2 Key through C2 Proficiency, plus the Young Learners suite, in our Miraflores building — with Cambridge-appointed speaking examiners and results issued directly by Cambridge University Press & Assessment.

  • Issuing body: Cambridge University Press & Assessment
  • Scope: A2 Key to C2 Proficiency, B1 Business Preliminary to C1 Business Higher, and the Young Learners suite
  • Valid until: 31 July 2027, subject to annual centre inspection

ETS — TOEFL iBT & TOEIC authorised venue

Educational Testing Service authorises our Miraflores building as a test venue for TOEFL iBT and for TOEIC Listening & Reading. Candidates register, prepare and sit in the same place, and score reports are issued by ETS directly to the universities or employers nominated on the day.

  • Issuing body: Educational Testing Service, Princeton
  • Scope: TOEFL iBT weekly; TOEIC Listening & Reading on demand, including in-company sittings
  • Valid until: 30 September 2027

IDP Education — IELTS registration partner

We are a registration partner for IELTS Academic and General Training with IDP Education, with computer-delivered sittings four times a month. Test Report Forms are issued by IDP and can be sent electronically to five receiving organisations at no charge.

  • Issuing body: IDP Education, in partnership with the British Council and Cambridge
  • Scope: IELTS Academic and General Training, computer-delivered, band scored
  • Valid until: 31 March 2028

Microsoft Learn for Educators

Official Microsoft courseware, instructor training and examination vouchers for the role-based certification path. Our Office, Power BI and Azure tracks are taught from Microsoft's own curriculum, and students sit MO-201, PL-300 and AZ-204 in our Pearson VUE laboratory.

  • Issuing body: Microsoft Corporation
  • Scope: Microsoft Office Specialist, Power Platform and Azure role-based certifications
  • Valid until: 30 June 2027, renewed annually

AWS Academy member institution

Membership of AWS Academy gives our cloud and machine learning tracks the official Amazon curriculum, accredited instructors and laboratory credits in live AWS accounts. Students receive discounted vouchers for Cloud Practitioner, Solutions Architect and Machine Learning Associate.

  • Issuing body: Amazon Web Services
  • Scope: Cloud Foundations, Cloud Architecting, Machine Learning Foundations and Data Engineering
  • Valid until: 31 December 2027

Cisco Networking Academy

A Cisco Networking Academy since 2011, with instructors holding CCNP and a physical rack laboratory alongside Packet Tracer. The CCNA path runs three times a year and carries the Academy discount on the certification examination itself.

  • Issuing body: Cisco Systems
  • Scope: CCNA Introduction to Networks, Switching and Routing, Enterprise Networking and Cybersecurity Essentials
  • Valid until: 28 February 2028

Adobe Certified curriculum

Our graphic design and motion tracks follow the Adobe Certified Professional curriculum for Photoshop, Illustrator, After Effects and Premiere Pro. Enrolled students receive licensed Creative Cloud access for the duration of the course and sit the certification on campus.

  • Issuing body: Adobe, examinations delivered by Certiport
  • Scope: Adobe Certified Professional in Visual Design, Digital Video and Graphic Design & Illustration
  • Valid until: 31 August 2027

We also hold academic partnerships with CompTIA, Oracle Academy, the Python Institute and the Linux Professional Institute, which supply courseware and discounted examination vouchers for the credentials listed in the index below. Registration numbers and current certificates of partnership are available for inspection at the academic registry, second floor, Av. José Larco 1301.

What your certificate looks like

A credential that proves itself

Every institutional certificate we issue is a signed digital record, not a scanned image. It carries a unique code, a QR verification link and a cryptographic hash anchored to a public blockchain, so an employer can confirm it in seconds without an email to our registry.

  • Blockchain-anchored — the certificate hash is written to a public ledger at issue, so any later alteration is detectable by anyone.
  • QR verification — printed on the PDF and embedded in the digital badge. Scanning it returns the holder, programme, grade, hours and issue date.
  • LinkedIn sharing — publish to your profile in two taps, with the credential ID and verification URL filled in automatically.
  • PDF and printed versions — a signed A4 PDF for applications, and an embossed printed original collected from the registry or posted anywhere in Perú within five working days.
  • Lifetime verification portal — institutional certificates never expire and remain downloadable from the virtual campus for life, including for alumni from 1994 onward.
  • Bilingual by default — issued in Spanish and English on the same document, so it needs no sworn translation for most applications abroad.

Frequently asked

Questions about certificates, licences and validity

The six questions the admissions office answers most often. If yours is not here, an adviser will answer it in an appointment or by telephone.

Is a Luis Urbiola certificate valid outside Perú?
Our institutional certificate is a Peruvian credential recognised under our MINEDU licence, and our degrees are valid under SUNEDU Resolution 148-2019. Abroad, recognition depends on the receiving country: many employers accept the bilingual certificate directly, while academic recognition usually requires apostille through the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, which our registry prepares on request. This is exactly why every technology and language programme also prepares you for an international certification — a Cambridge, IELTS, AWS or Cisco credential is recognised identically in every market.
What is the difference between the institutional certificate and the international one?
The institutional certificate is issued by us and records what you studied: programme, contact hours, modules, grade and completion date. The international certification is issued by an external awarding body — Cambridge, ETS, IDP, Microsoft, Amazon, Cisco, Adobe, Oracle or CompTIA — and records that you passed their standardised examination on a specific date. Every flagship programme pairs the two, and the fees in the table above cover only the second.
Do I have to sit the examination at Luis Urbiola?
No, but most students do. We are an authorised Cambridge centre PE-084, an ETS venue for TOEFL iBT and TOEIC, an IDP registration partner for IELTS and a Pearson VUE and PSI laboratory for vendor examinations — so preparation, registration and the sitting itself all happen in the Miraflores building. If you prefer another venue or another country, we register you there instead and your preparation course is unaffected.
How long does each certification stay valid?
Cambridge English qualifications, Adobe Certified Professional at the professional tier, the Figma certificate, PCEP and Oracle OCA do not expire. IELTS, TOEFL and TOEIC scores are conventionally accepted for two years. AWS, Cisco and CompTIA credentials run for three years and are renewed by recertification or continuing-education credits. Microsoft role-based certifications are valid for one year and renew free of charge through a short online assessment. The validity column in the table above is authoritative for the 2026 academic year.
Can an employer verify my certificate without contacting me?
Yes. Each certificate carries a code in the format LU-YYYY-NNNNN and a QR panel that resolves to the verification service on this page. The lookup returns the holder's name, programme, result, contact hours and issue date, and nothing else — no address, no identity document, no payment history. Verification is free, unlimited and does not require the employer to register or to notify you.
I graduated years ago and lost my certificate. Can I get another copy?
Alumni keep virtual campus access for life, and every certificate issued from 2015 onward can be redownloaded there at any time at no charge. For documents issued between 1994 and 2014, the academic registry reissues from the physical archive within five working days for an administrative fee of S/ 60. Bring or send your DNI or passport number and the approximate year of study to admisiones@luisurbiola.edu.pe.
