Academic calendar

The year ahead, month by month

Ten fixed milestones between August 2026 and July 2027. Dates for individual courses appear in your virtual campus timetable once you are enrolled.

  1. August 2026

    Open day season and second-semester enrolment

    Open days at Miraflores and San Isidro, free level tests every Saturday, and enrolment for the second semester closes on 28 August.

  2. September 2026

    Second semester begins · Andean AI Conference

    Classes resume on 1 September across all seven campuses. The conference runs on the 3rd and 4th, followed by the fintech hackathon and the English graduation.

  3. October 2026

    Mid-term assessment and regional open days

    Mid-term examinations run from 19 to 24 October. Arequipa opens its doors on the 3rd; the Cybersecurity Summit takes the auditorium on the 15th.

  4. November 2026

    Employability Forum and the climate datathon

    Our largest recruitment event of the year on 20 November, with 212 hiring partners. Trujillo hosts both the datathon and its own open day.

  5. December 2026

    Final examinations and university graduation

    Finals from 1 to 10 December, graduation on the 12th, and enrolment opens for the January summer intensives. Campuses close from 24 December to 1 January.

  6. January 2027

    Summer intensives

    Four-week accelerated courses in English, Python and data analytics, weekdays from 08:00 to 12:00. One CEFR level completed in a single month.

  7. February 2027

    Entrance examinations and admissions deadline

    University entrance examinations on 6, 13 and 20 February. Applications for the March 2027 intake close on 26 February with no extension.

  8. March 2027

    Academic year opens · Welcome Week

    Classes begin on 8 March. Welcome Week runs from the 1st: campus tours, virtual campus training, society fair and the rector's address.

  9. April 2027

    Research Week and the Faculty Lecture Series

    Undergraduate research posters, twelve public lectures from visiting academics, and the annual teaching prize awarded on 30 April.

  10. July 2027

    Anniversary week — thirty-three years

    Founded in 1994 by Luis Alberto Urbiola. A week of alumni reunions, open lectures and the mid-year graduation ceremony on 17 July.
