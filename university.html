Academic calendar 2026

Key dates

Two undergraduate intakes and three postgraduate intakes per year. Applications close four weeks before each start date.

  • 12 JANUARY

    Applications open — March intake

    Scholarship assessments run in parallel.

  • 28 FEBRUARY

    Application deadline

    Entrance examination and interviews the following week.

  • 16 MARCH

    Semester I begins

    Orientation week runs 9–13 March.

  • 10 JULY

    Semester I ends · Winter graduation

    Ceremony at the Miraflores auditorium.

  • 10 AUGUST

    Semester II begins

    Second undergraduate intake.

  • 18 DECEMBER

    Semester II ends · Summer graduation

    Thesis defences 1–12 December.
