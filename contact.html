Academic advising appointment

A structured half hour with an adviser from the school you are interested in. It is not a sales call: roughly half our advising sessions end with a recommendation to start later, start at a different level, or study something else entirely.

What happens in the session

  • Ten minutes on where you are now: studies, work, schedule, budget and the deadline you are working towards
  • A placement check where relevant — the adaptive English level test or the technology diagnostic, both twenty-five minutes and both free
  • A written pathway: programmes, sequence, total months, total cost and the instalment plan that fits your income
  • A scholarship pre-assessment against the six aid programmes, so you know your likely discount before you commit
  • A campus tour of the classrooms, laboratories and library if you are meeting us in person

What to bring

Your national identity document or passport is enough for a first conversation. If you already hold qualifications you want recognised — a secondary-school certificate, a technical diploma, a foreign degree, an English certificate — bring a photograph or scan and the adviser can give you an indicative credit recognition on the spot. Applicants under eighteen should come with a parent or guardian, or have them join the video call.

On campus or by video

Both formats run to the same script and carry the same weight. On-campus sessions take place at any of the seven campuses; video sessions run on Google Meet and the link arrives by WhatsApp fifteen minutes beforehand. If you need an interpreter for Quechua or sign language, note it in the message field and we will arrange one at no cost with three working days of notice.

Confirmation within the hour Requests sent during opening hours are confirmed by WhatsApp within sixty minutes. Requests sent overnight are confirmed before 08:30 the next working day.

Frequently asked questions

The twelve questions our advisers answer most often, written out in full so you do not have to ask them at all.

How do I enrol, and how long does it take?
Enrolment is five steps and, for short courses, can be completed in a single afternoon. Submit the application form, attend a free thirty-minute advising session, take the relevant assessment — the adaptive English level test, the technology diagnostic or the university entrance examination — upload your documents to the applicant portal, then pay the first instalment and receive your campus credentials. Short courses are usually confirmed within forty-eight hours; degree programmes take up to seven working days because the entrance examination and interview must be scheduled. You can also enrol entirely over WhatsApp on +51 987 442 019 if you already know which programme you want.
Which payment methods do you accept?
Visa and Mastercard, both credit and debit, through the secure payment link we send by email or WhatsApp. Yape and Plin to our institutional number, with the operation code sent back to finance. Bank transfer or direct deposit to our accounts at BCP, Interbank and BBVA — always include your DNI number as the reference so the payment is matched automatically. In-person payment in cash or by card at the cashier's desk of any of the seven campuses. We do not accept cheques. Electronic receipts and invoices are issued within twenty-four hours of the payment clearing; companies requiring a factura should send their RUC to corporativo@luisurbiola.edu.pe before paying.
What is your refund policy?
If you withdraw before the first session, you receive a full refund of everything paid, with no administrative charge. Withdraw during the first week and 80% is refunded. Withdraw during the second week and 50% is refunded. From the third week onwards no refund is made, but you may transfer the remaining value to a later intake once, within twelve months. If we cancel a course — for example because a group does not reach the minimum size — you are refunded in full within five working days or moved to another group or timetable at your choice. Refund requests go to finance on extension 185 and are settled to the original payment method within ten working days.
Can I change my schedule once the course has started?
Yes, subject to places being available in the group you want. One free schedule change is included per course; further changes carry a S/ 40 administrative fee. Requests go to the academic office on extension 120 and are normally processed within two working days. You may switch between morning, evening and Saturday groups, and between on-campus and online-live delivery, provided you remain at the same level. Changing level after the course has begun requires a short reassessment with your instructor, which is free.
When and how do I receive my certificate?
Digital certificates with a verification code and QR are issued within ten working days of your final grade being published, and are sent to the email registered on your file. Printed certificates are ready for collection at the reception of your campus within twenty working days, or can be couriered anywhere in Perú for S/ 25 and internationally for S/ 140. Cambridge, IELTS, TOEFL and TOEIC certificates follow the awarding body's own timetable — usually four to eight weeks — and are delivered to us first, then handed over at reception against your identity document. Duplicate certificates for graduates are requested through alumni relations on extension 195.
Is an online course worth the same as an on-campus one?
Yes. Online-live programmes follow the same syllabus, the same number of contact hours, the same assessments and the same instructors as their on-campus equivalents, and the certificate makes no distinction between the two. What differs is the delivery: sessions run on our virtual campus with breakout rooms, and every session is recorded and available for thirty days. Practical laboratory work in technology programmes still requires attendance in person or a supervised remote laboratory session; your adviser will tell you which weeks these fall in before you enrol. Students routinely move between the two modes mid-programme without any loss of credit.
Is there a minimum age?
Our Kids programmes start at five years old and our Teens Academy runs from twelve to seventeen. Applicants under eighteen enrol with the signature of a parent or legal guardian, who also receives access to the parent dashboard and the weekly progress report. Adult general English, technology short courses and professional programmes require a minimum age of sixteen. Bachelor degrees require completed secondary education and, in practice, applicants are sixteen or older. There is no upper age limit of any kind, and our oldest current student is seventy-eight, enrolled in B1 English.
What scholarships exist and how do I apply?
There are six programmes: Academic Excellence up to 100%, First in Family up to 75%, Regional Talent up to 60%, Single-Parent Support up to 50%, Sport and Culture up to 50%, and Corporate Sponsorship up to 40%. There is no separate application form — every applicant is assessed automatically when they apply, and the result is communicated with the admission decision. Two in five of our students receive some form of support. Scholarships are renewed each semester provided you maintain the stated average and attendance. Employees of our 212 partner companies receive between 20% and 40% off independently of the scholarship programmes; ask your human resources team or write to corporativo@luisurbiola.edu.pe.
How large are the classes?
General English groups are capped at sixteen students, exam-preparation groups at ten to twelve, Conversation Club at eight, Kids at ten, and technology laboratory sessions at one student per workstation with a maximum of twenty. University lectures vary by faculty but seminars and workshops are capped at twenty-five. If a group falls below six students we either merge it with another group at the same level, move it to a nearby timetable, or refund in full — we never run an under-filled group at a lower service level without telling you first.
What happens if I miss a class?
Every session across every programme is recorded and published to the virtual campus within two hours of finishing, and stays available for thirty days. Recordings do not count towards attendance, though: certificates require 75% live attendance for short courses and 70% for degree programmes, in line with SUNEDU rules. If illness, work travel or family circumstances take you below that threshold, contact the academic office on extension 120 before the end of the course — documented absences can be excused, and where they cannot, we will place you in a later group at no additional charge. Missing an examination requires a medical certificate or equivalent evidence within three working days to qualify for a resit.
Is there parking at the campuses?
Miraflores headquarters has an underground car park with 62 spaces, free for the first two hours with validation at reception. Surco has 90 surface spaces and Los Olivos has 140, both free for students and visitors. San Isidro has 45 spaces with a S/ 5 flat rate because of municipal restrictions on Javier Prado. San Juan de Lurigancho, Arequipa and Trujillo all have free parking with no space limit in practice. Secure bicycle racks are available at every campus, and motorcycle parking at all except San Isidro. Evening classes are the busiest period, so arrive fifteen minutes early if you are driving between 18:00 and 19:00.
Are the campuses accessible?
All seven campuses have step-free entrances, lifts to every teaching floor and accessible toilets on each level. Miraflores, San Isidro and Los Olivos have induction loops in the main lecture halls. We provide sign-language interpreting for classes and for advising appointments free of charge with three working days of notice, and materials can be supplied in large print, in accessible digital formats compatible with screen readers, or in braille on request. Examination adjustments — extra time, a separate room, a reader or a scribe — are arranged through student services on extension 135 at least ten working days before the sitting. If any part of a building or programme is not currently accessible to you, tell us and we will move the class rather than move you.
