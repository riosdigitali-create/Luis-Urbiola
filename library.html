Miraflores headquarters

Four floors, and a chair with your name on it

The digital collection is the larger half of the library, but it is not the whole of it. Av. José Larco 1301 holds four floors of reading rooms, printed reserve stock and the quietest study space in the district.

  • Four floors — ground floor for periodicals and returns, first for reserve stock, second and third for silent study.
  • 320 study seats — every desk with power, and 96 of them bookable in advance from the student portal.
  • 24 group rooms — four to eight people, whiteboard and screen, free in two-hour blocks.
  • Research help desk — three librarians on the second floor, no appointment needed, plus scheduled clinics before every submission deadline.
  • Alumni are welcome — bring your graduate card to reception and the turnstile opens, thirty-two years after you left.
