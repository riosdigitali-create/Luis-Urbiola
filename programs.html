Not sure where to start?

Five questions decide almost every academic pathway

Our admissions advisers ask the same five questions in every appointment. Answer them here and you will already know which of the three schools to open first.

The five questions

  • QUESTION 1

    What is your English level right now?

    Below B1 and English comes first — every technical and postgraduate reading list assumes B2. The free 25-minute level test gives you a formal CEFR report in 24 hours.

  • QUESTION 2

    Do you need a credential or a skill?

    A credential means an examination date and an issuing body: Cambridge, IELTS, AWS, Cisco. A skill means a portfolio. Most students eventually want both, in that order.

  • QUESTION 3

    How many hours can you protect each week?

    Six hours supports one flagship programme. Twelve supports two, or the intensive track. Fewer than four and the Saturday-only formats are the honest answer.

  • QUESTION 4

    Is the goal a job, a promotion or a licence?

    A first job favours a technology track plus B2 English. A promotion favours an executive certificate. A regulated profession requires the licensed university degree, and nothing else substitutes for it.

  • QUESTION 5

    Campus, online or both?

    Every programme runs in Miraflores, live online, or a hybrid of the two at the same price. You can switch format between terms without reapplying.
