Working here

What we offer the people who teach and support 50,000 graduates

  • Free study for you and your household — any programme in any of the three schools, including postgraduate.
  • Formal contracts from day one — EsSalud, CTS, gratifications and paid holiday, without probation-period exceptions.
  • Funded research and certification — up to S/ 9,000 a year towards doctoral study, conferences or vendor certifications.
  • Protected preparation time — teaching loads capped at 18 contact hours a week so that classes stay prepared.
  • Internal mobility — 41% of our 2025 appointments were existing colleagues moving school or campus.

How selection works

Application review within ten working days · a competency interview · a taught class or technical exercise for academic and technical posts · a panel with the school director · offer and reference checks. The whole process takes four to five weeks and every applicant receives a written outcome.
